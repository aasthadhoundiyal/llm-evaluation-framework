LEARNFUND FINANCIAL LOANS, FEES, AND SECURED ASSETS GUIDE
=========================================================

1. Personal Loans:
- Unsecured fixed-rate loans ranging from $2,500 to $50,000.
- Repayment Terms: Available loan terms of 24, 36, 48, and 60 months.
- Annual Percentage Rates (APR): From 6.99% to 23.49% with autopay discount of 0.25% included.
- Origination Fee: 1.0% to 5.0% deducted directly from the gross loan proceeds upon disbursement.
- Prepayment Penalty: $0. Borrowers may pay down balance or settle loan early without any fee.

2. Auto Loans:
- New Vehicle Purchase: Financing terms up to 72 months starting at 4.49% APR.
- Used Vehicle Purchase: Financing up to 60 months starting at 5.29% APR.
- Loan-to-Value (LTV): Maximum allowable LTV is 115% of NADA retail guide value.

3. Standard Service Fees:
- Domestic Outgoing Wire: $25 per wire.
- International Outgoing Wire: $45 per wire.
- Overdraft Paid Item Fee: $35 per item (maximum 3 fees per calendar day).
- Stop Payment Order: $30 per request valid for 6 months.
- Paper Statement Fee: $3 per month (waived with Paperless e-Statements enrollment).
- Cashier's Check: $10 (free for Premier Tier checking account holders).
