LEARNFUND FINANCIAL CREDIT CARD PRODUCT GUIDE (2026 EDITION)
============================================================

1. Platinum Cash Rewards Card:
- Introductory APR: 0% APR for the first 15 billing cycles on purchases and eligible balance transfers.
- Standard Variable APR: 18.24% to 28.24% variable based on prime credit rating.
- Cash Back Rewards: 3% cash back on groceries and dining up to $6,000 annually, 2% on wholesale clubs and gas stations, and 1% unlimited cash back on all other purchases.
- Annual Fee: $0.
- Foreign Transaction Fee: 3% of the U.S. dollar amount of each transaction.

2. Premier Travel Advantage Card:
- Sign-up Bonus: 60,000 bonus points after spending $4,000 in qualifying purchases within the first 90 days of account opening.
- Point Multipliers: 5x points on flights booked directly with airlines, 3x points on hotels and rental cars, 1x on general spending.
- Annual Fee: $95 billed on your first statement cycle.
- Foreign Transaction Fee: $0 (No foreign exchange fee).
- Travel Insurance: Includes complimentary collision damage waiver for vehicle rentals up to $50,000 and baggage delay reimbursement up to $500.

3. Secure Start Credit Builder Card:
- Designed for individuals looking to establish or rebuild personal credit history.
- Security Deposit: Minimum refundable security deposit of $200 up to a maximum of $2,500. Credit line equals the security deposit submitted.
- Automatic Review: Accounts are evaluated automatically after 7 consecutive months of on-time payments for potential upgrade to an unsecured credit line.
- Annual Fee: $0.
- Standard APR: Fixed 22.99%.
